# Bundled fonts

Both families are redistributable, and both are embedded in the shipped `.pbw`
subset to the handful of glyphs the face actually draws. Attribution and
licence terms therefore travel with the watchface, not just with this repo.

| File | Family | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Montserrat-Bold.ttf`, `Montserrat-Light.ttf` | Montserrat | SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`OFL-Montserrat.txt`) |
| `Roboto-Bold.ttf`, `Roboto-Light.ttf` | Roboto | Apache License 2.0 (`LICENSE-2.0-Roboto.txt`) |
