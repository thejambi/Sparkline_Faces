Your hour drawn as landscape - and last night's sleep, before you get up.

The bottom of the screen is the last sixty minutes: one column a minute, standing on the very bottom edge, with a quarter-hour graticule behind it and a dotted line at walking pace. Above it is the clock, as large as a 200-pixel screen will allow. Between them is one warm line: the horizon.

Before you get up, the face shows you the night instead. Last night's sleep holds the step slot until you have actually moved - 350 steps by default, and adjustable. While it does, the chart's window becomes the night itself: sixty columns from the moment you fell asleep to the moment you woke, each one how much you moved, scaled so the most restless stretch fills the plot. The whole face runs cool until you cross that threshold, then warms the moment steps take the slot back.

* Two layouts
* Six built-in themes, plus full custom colors
* Four clock faces, including a real seven-segment display and Blocky Digits - not a typeface at all, but drawn pixel by pixel for this watchface. The rest of the type is selectable too
* Panels that can stay off the screen until you shake your wrist, and a clock that takes the freed width as size for the seven seconds they are away
* Steps, the day's distance, your heart rate, the date, the temperature, and a two-pixel battery bar along the very top edge
* Weather from Open-Meteo: no account and no API key. It follows your phone, or a city or postal code you type in, in which case it never touches phone location at all

Built for Pebble Time 2 - every number in the layout is chosen for its 200x228 color screen - and derived again for Pebble Time and Time Steel, Pebble 2 and Pebble 2 Duo.
